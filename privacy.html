Superforce VR Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Superforce VR is a virtual reality game for Meta Quest. This policy explains what information the game and its online room server collect, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how to ask for it to be deleted.

In short

  • We don't ask for your real name, email address, age, phone number, home address or location.
  • Online play uses a player name that you choose and a random player ID that the game makes on your headset.
  • Voice chat goes live to the other players in your room. It is never recorded or stored.
  • Purchases are handled by Meta. We never see your payment details.
  • There are no ads and no analytics, and we never sell or rent any information.

Information kept on your headset

These are saved only on your Quest and are never uploaded:

  • Your progress and settings: Sup Bucks, Super Gems, powers, cosmetics, game settings, your player name, your random player ID and whether you are banned from online rooms.
  • A small note about whether the game closed properly last time, used only to show a message after a crash.
  • Videos you record with the in-game camera (the beta Recording feature), saved in your headset's Movies/Superforce folder.

Uninstalling the game deletes this information. Recorded videos stay in Movies/Superforce until you delete them, for example with the Files app.

Information the room server receives when you play online

Our room server connects players in online rooms. When you play online it receives:

  • Your player name. Other players in your room see it over your head. You can change it at any time on the computer in your apartment (NAME TAG).
  • Your player ID. A random code that the game makes on your headset. We use it only to keep bans and moderator roles working, and we do not link it to your Meta account.
  • Your game version, so players on older versions can be told to update.
  • Gameplay data: your position, head and hand movements, cosmetics and the powers you use. This is passed on live to the other players in your room and is not stored.
  • Voice: when your microphone is on, your voice is passed on live to the players in your room. The server does not record, store or listen to it.
  • Your internet (IP) address, which every connection needs. If a player on an old game version is removed from a room, their address is kept in the server's memory for 10 minutes so they can't rejoin that room straight away. It is not saved to disk.
  • Your Meta user ID (Meta Store version only). To check whether a player is the game's owner, the game sends its Meta user ID and a one-time proof from Meta. The server compares the ID with the owner's. Only when it matches is it sent to Meta to confirm. It is not stored.

Information the room server keeps

  • Recently seen players: player names and their player IDs, so moderators can act on a player by name. Only the most recent few thousand are kept, and older entries drop off.
  • Bans: the player ID, player name and ban length, until the ban ends or is lifted.
  • Moderators: the player ID and player name, until the role is removed.
  • Rooms: room codes and who is in a room, only while the room is open.

The game owner's headset keeps a copy of the ban and moderator lists so they can be put back if the server restarts.

Who information is shared with

  • Other players in your room see your player name, your character and its movements, and hear your voice.
  • Render, the company that hosts our room server. Like most hosts, it may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for a short time.
  • Meta. Purchases (such as the Web Slinger Pack and Gem packs) go through Meta's store. Meta handles payment and tells the game which items you own. Meta also confirms the game owner's identity as described above. Meta's own privacy policy covers what Meta collects.

We don't share information with anyone else.

How we use information

Only to run the game and its online play: putting you in rooms, showing your name, passing on movement and voice, keeping bans and moderation working, checking purchases and confirming the game owner. We don't use it for advertising or profiling.

Children

The game doesn't ask for anyone's age or contact details. Player names are seen by other players, so please don't use your real name. A parent or guardian can contact us at any time to have a child's information deleted.

Deleting your information

  • On your headset: uninstall the game, and delete any recorded videos from Movies/Superforce.
  • On the room server: email us with the player name you used. We will delete your player name, player ID and any ban or moderator entries within 30 days, and tell you when it's done.

Changes to this policy

If what the game collects changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
